Bonus — works in progress & local utilities

Fun experiments and personal Stata scripts that live in my adopath but aren't packaged on GitHub or SSC. They sit in a shared Google Drive folder — click a thumbnail (or the button) to browse and grab what's useful.

blackjack.ado — Stata 21: a full blackjack game in the Results window (shuffled multi-deck shoe; double, split, surrender, insurance; 3:2 blackjacks).
itunes.ado — drive Apple Music (or legacy iTunes) from Stata: a clickable play / pause / skip / shuffle / volume panel.
est_pi.ado — estimate π by Monte Carlo: scatter random points in a square and count the share that land inside the circle.
